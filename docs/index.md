## Apologies

For you have found yourself in a corner of the internet that has little aim besides that of reflecting the creator. Overly dramatic prose, crude imagery and an aimless rage. Though that’s not to say that all on offer is dour musings. The occasional smile shall be spared to soak up the hateful tears. 

As time marches on, so shall the word count. Perhaps we will even live to see the supposed refinement of these opulent thoughts. Read on or don’t. It hardly matters. 

Thanks for visiting!
